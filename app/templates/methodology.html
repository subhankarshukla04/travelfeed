methodology

Built backwards from twenty issues of Travel Monthly.

The source list, section taxonomy, and scoring weights here are reverse-engineered from every issue of Travel Monthly Nedyalko Terziev published between August 2017 and April 2019 — twenty issues, ~160 stories, ~70 distinct publications cited. The premise is simple: if a working VP of Strategy spent two years curating a beat, the beat is already defined. This site only has to follow it.

how the source list was chosen

Every publication cited more than once across the twenty issues is in. Trade press came first (Skift, PhocusWire, Web in Travel, Travolution, TTG Asia, Travel Daily Media). Specialty desks followed (CAPA, The Air Current, Hospitality Net, The Company Dime). Premium business is in for the M&A and capital-flow stories that drive the sector (Bloomberg, Reuters, FT, WSJ, The Information). Regional anchors handle MENA (Arabian Business, Khaleej Times, The National, Arab News, Saudi Gazette, MENABytes) and APAC (Channel News Asia, DealStreetAsia, Tech in Asia, Nikkei Asia, Straits Times). India is its own lane via Economic Times and Mint.

Sources he didn't cite are not here. No general consumer travel media, no aggregator blogs, no PR wires. The 20-issue archive lives on the /archive page — every reconstructed digest from May 2019 onward follows the same source pattern.

section taxonomy

The seven sections — Travel Tech, Airlines, Hoteliers, M&A, Regulatory, Emerging, Executive — are the recurring columns Ned organised every issue around. M&A always led when the deal was big; Executive moves anchored the back half. Emerging covered frontier tech (blockchain then; AI agents now). The taxonomy is unchanged.

scoring

Each story scores on five axes. Source tier reflects which publications Ned cited most (Skift, PhocusWire, CAPA carry the most weight). Region weight skews toward MENA, APAC, and South Asia — the three growth corridors Wego operates in. Company match boosts stories that name a tracked operator. Capital signal flags M&A, IPO, funding, and commission stories — the ones that move strategy, not just news. Recency decays linearly over thirty days. 

score = source_tier        (1–25)
      + theme_match       (0–25)
      + region_boost      (0–15  · MENA / APAC / South Asia weighted up)
      + company_match     (0–15  · tracked travel operators)
      + capital_signal    (+10   · M&A · IPO · funding · commission)
      + recency           (0–10  · 30-day linear)

tracked operators

The right rail pins the Wego ecosystem: Wego, Almosafer, Seera, Tajawal, Riyadh Air, MakeMyTrip, ixigo, IndiGo, Klook, Trip.com. These aren't "trending" — they're the operators a Wego BD reader watches every day. The count next to each is mention volume in the last 30 days; a low count means a quiet month, not a deprioritisation.

how reading works

Every headline links straight to the publisher. No content is excerpted, scraped, or hosted here. If you subscribe to Skift, Bloomberg, the FT, or any other paywalled source, your subscription works exactly as it does anywhere else — travelfeed is the index, the publishers are where you read.

what it isn't

Not a summariser. Not an AI commentator. No analytics, no login, no newsletter signup. The editorial judgment lives in the source list and the scoring weights — not in generated text.
