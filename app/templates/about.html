about

Built by Subhankar Shukla as a working artifact for a Wego BD conversation.

I'm an NUS NOC candidate interviewing with Nedyalko Terziev for a BD role at Wego. Rather than send a deck, I built the thing I'd actually want to use on the job — a daily index of the travel-tech beat, scored for the kind of stories a strategy reader notices first.

why it exists

Ned ran Travel Monthly from August 2017 through April 2019 — twenty issues of carefully sourced travel-industry analysis, ending after he joined Wego. The discipline of the series (consistent sections, repeat publications, capital-flow emphasis, MENA + APAC focus) is the most legible map of what a Wego strategy reader actually pays attention to.

Every source on this site was cited in that archive. The taxonomy and scoring weights were reverse-engineered from it. The /archive page reproduces all twenty original issues and reconstructs every month since — same structure, real events, authentic source attribution.

honest disclosures

  • ·  The 13 issues marked published on the timeline are extracted from Ned's LinkedIn Pulse and Medium archive. Headlines and openers are his.
  • ·  The 91 issues marked reconstructed are not Ned's — they are my reconstructions of what a Travel Monthly issue might have looked like for that month, anchored on real travel-industry events I sourced separately. Treat them as a historical reading list, not as his work.
  • ·  Every headline links out to the publisher. No content is hosted, scraped, or excerpted here.
  • ·  The live feed pulls from RSS only; nothing is paid, nothing is sponsored.

stack

Flask · Postgres · feedparser · Tailwind · Alpine. Hosted locally during the interview window; deployable in one command. The full methodology walks through the editorial choices.
