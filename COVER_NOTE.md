Before our chat, I built something you didn't ask for and I want to give it to you anyway.

It's a news feed for Wego. Not a generic travel feed — Phocuswire and Skift Pro already exist and you almost certainly have them open. This one is different in three specific ways:

**It's calibrated to Wego's competitive set, not the industry's.** When I sort by "competitor moves," I get Almosafer, Tajawal, MakeMyTrip, ixigo, Traveloka, Trip.com — the people you actually compete with for a booking. Not Marriott's CFO commentary.

**It weights regions the way your revenue does — MENA 65 / India 20 / SEA 15.** Phocuswire is a US-skewed publication. The default view here surfaces the GCC / India outbound / SE-Asia stories first because that's where Wego makes money.

**It separates capital-flow signal from operational news.** The "capital" lens shows me only M&A, funding, partnerships — the moves that matter for a Strategy & Planning function. Most travel feeds bury those under hotel openings and route launches.

The artifact you're looking at — `MONDAY_BRIEF.md` — is what falls out of that feed at the end of a week, hand-curated, with one line of "implication for Wego" on each item. About 30 minutes to produce. About 5 minutes to read.

1. **ixigo just shipped three ChatGPT apps last week.** I have no idea whether Wego has a position on this and that's exactly the kind of question I think this brief should be raising weekly, not quarterly.

I am not pitching this as a product.
